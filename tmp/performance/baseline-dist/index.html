5 questions in All Questions

1.Is AGAI only for athletes?

No. Alongside sports rehabilitation and conditioning, AGAI offers orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation, women’s fitness and wellness, pelvic floor rehabilitation and care for older adults.

2.When should I consider physiotherapy?

If pain, stiffness, an injury or difficulty moving is affecting everyday activities, a physiotherapy assessment can help you understand your options. You can also ask about rehabilitation after surgery or support with returning to activity.

3.Who will guide my care?

Dr. Rasathi Rajendren, PT, is the founder and sports physiotherapist at AGAI. She will discuss your concerns and plan care around your needs and goals.

4.How is my rehabilitation programme planned?

The approach combines clinical assessment, hands-on therapy, individualised exercise programmes and patient education. Your needs and movement goals guide the plan.

5.What if I am unsure which service to choose?

You can explain your question in your own words in the contact enquiry form. You do not need to choose a specific rehabilitation service before making an enquiry.
