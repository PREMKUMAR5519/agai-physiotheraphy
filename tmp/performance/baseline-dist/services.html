Sports & performance

Sports injury prevention & rehabilitation

For an injury that is keeping you away from sport or exercise.

When to consider this care

A sprain, strain or sports injury is limiting training, exercise or play.

We assess how your injury affects movement and plan rehabilitation around the activity you want to return to.

How we can support you

  • Understanding your injury and movement
  • Rebuilding strength and control
  • Planning a gradual return to activity

Sports & performance

Speed, strength & conditioning

For building strength, stamina and confidence in exercise.

When to consider this care

You want a structured exercise plan suited to your fitness or sporting goals.

Purposeful exercise programmes centred on speed, strength and physical conditioning, with your individual goals guiding the approach.

How we can support you

  • Exercises suited to your starting point
  • Strength and stamina training
  • Progressing towards your activity goals
Book Appointment
Rehabilitation

Orthopaedic rehabilitation

For back, neck or joint pain, stiffness and recovery after surgery.

When to consider this care

Pain or stiffness makes sitting, bending, lifting or climbing stairs difficult, or you need rehabilitation after surgery.

Care for problems affecting muscles, bones and joints. We assess your movement and discuss exercises, advice and hands-on care where appropriate.

How we can support you

  • Understanding what makes movement difficult
  • Exercises for mobility and strength
  • Support with everyday activities
Book Appointment
Rehabilitation

Neurological rehabilitation

For movement and balance difficulties linked to a neurological condition.

When to consider this care

An existing neurological condition is affecting your walking, balance or daily tasks.

Rehabilitation for people whose movement is affected by conditions involving the brain, spinal cord or nerves, including recovery after a stroke.

How we can support you

  • Practising movements used in daily life
  • Working on balance and coordination
  • Setting achievable rehabilitation goals
Book Appointment
Women’s wellness

Women’s clinical fitness & wellness

For movement, strength and exercise support through different life stages.

When to consider this care

You want help with movement or a supported return to exercise during or after pregnancy.

Individual physiotherapy and exercise guidance for women, including during pregnancy and after childbirth, based on an assessment of your needs.

How we can support you

  • Discussing your comfort and activity goals
  • Exercises adapted to your needs
  • Support during and after pregnancy
Book Appointment
Women’s wellness

Pelvic floor rehabilitation

For concerns such as bladder leakage or pelvic floor weakness.

When to consider this care

Bladder leakage or concerns about pelvic floor strength are affecting your comfort or confidence.

A sensitive, individual assessment helps us understand your concerns and discuss whether pelvic floor exercises and guidance may help.

How we can support you

  • Time to discuss your concerns
  • Understanding pelvic floor function
  • An individual exercise and care plan
Book Appointment
Healthy ageing

Geriatric rehabilitation

For older adults finding walking, balance or everyday movement harder.

When to consider this care

Getting up from a chair, walking or keeping your balance has become more difficult as you get older.

Care focused on the movements that support daily independence, with exercises adapted to your abilities and goals.

How we can support you

  • Strength for everyday tasks
  • Walking and balance practice
  • Building confidence with movement
