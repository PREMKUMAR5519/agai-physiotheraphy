Your first visit. A little clarity before you arrive.

What to bring, what to ask, and how to make space for a conversation about your care.

Start with your story

You do not need the perfect words to explain why you are coming in. Think about what brought you here, the activities you would like to get back to, and the questions you want to ask. A few notes on your phone or a piece of paper can help you remember what matters to you.

Bring what helps the conversation

If you have existing reports, referral letters or information about previous treatment, keep them together so you can discuss them with your physiotherapist. Ask the clinic beforehand if there is anything specific to bring or wear for your appointment.

Leave room for questions

You can ask what the next steps involve, how appointments are arranged and who to contact between visits. Before you leave, check that you understand any instructions you have been given. It is always okay to ask for something to be explained again.
