Coming along? How to be a thoughtful support.

Make space for the person receiving care while helping with the practical details.

IN THIS ARTICLE

Ask what would be helpfulMake space for their voiceAgree on the practical next stepsAll articles

Ask what would be helpful

If you are accompanying a family member or friend, ask what support they would like. They may want help with travel, remembering questions or keeping appointment details organised. Let their preferences guide your role.

Make space for their voice

Give the person receiving care room to describe their own goals and experiences. Ask before adding information or taking notes. You can check with the clinic in advance about bringing someone to the appointment.

Agree on the practical next steps

Before leaving, check which details the person would like help remembering. This might include a follow-up date or a contact number. Any questions about care instructions should go back to the clinician for clarification.

General information to help you prepare for a conversation. Your physiotherapist can advise on your individual care.
