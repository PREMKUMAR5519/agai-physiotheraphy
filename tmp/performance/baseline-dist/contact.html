1.Does submitting an enquiry book an appointment?

No. Your enquiry is sent to the centre for review. An appointment is confirmed only after you hear back from the centre.

2.What should I include in my message?

Your name, a way to contact you, the service you are interested in and a brief question or preferred appointment day are a useful starting point.

3.Can I enquire without choosing a service?

Yes. Simply explain what you would like help with in your message. You do not need to choose a service before getting in touch.
