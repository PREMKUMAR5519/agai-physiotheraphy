About AGAI

Based in Chennai.
Focused on your movement.

We are AGAI Physiotherapy Centre, based in Ambattur, Chennai. We help people with pain, stiffness, injuries and movement difficulties work towards everyday activities and the things they enjoy.

Our services include sports injury rehabilitation, orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation, women’s fitness and wellness, pelvic floor rehabilitation, and care for older adults.

Care starts with a conversation.

We listen to your concerns, assess your movement and discuss care options with you. Your daily routine and goals help guide the next step.
