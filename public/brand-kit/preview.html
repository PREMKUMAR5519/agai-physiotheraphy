02 / Space and size

Leave space around the visible artwork.

Let H be the height of the gold shield. Keep clear space equal to H/10 on every side, measured from the outermost visible edge of the logo.

Use the padded exports

The deployment files include the minimum margin. The exact source files retain their original canvases; allow the margin in your layout when placing a source file.

Do not crop the descriptor, side rules, leaf or gold edge. Preserve the original proportions whenever resizing.

Choose a suitable background

Use white or a quiet pale surface. On dark colors and photographs, place the full-color mark inside a white panel with the same clear space.

Keep the gradients and lettering intact. Do not recolor, stretch, add shadows, outline or rearrange the logo.

03 / Color

Blue and green anchor the palette.

Use gold and lime sparingly outside the logo. Click any swatch to copy its HEX value. The PDF also lists RGB values and text contrast ratios.

Use white text on blue or green. Use dark ink on gold and lime. Avoid communicating status by color alone: include a label or recognizable icon.

Flat gold is a supporting layout color, not a replacement for the metallic gradient. For printed work, confirm the paper and color conversion with the printer.

04 / Typography

Inter for content and interface text.

The custom wordmark remains artwork. Use regular 400 for reading, semibold 600 for controls and bold 700 for headings. Keep headings and labels in sentence case.

05 / Applications

Website and printed layouts.

Use the horizontal signature where it fits at full size. On narrow screens, the shield with separate readable name text keeps the header compact.

Digital layouts

1120 px maximum content width; 32 px desktop and 20 px mobile gutters. Use an 8 px spacing rhythm, 48 px controls, persistent form labels and visible keyboard focus.

Use WebP for compact website images, SVG for scalable artwork, and PNG for tools without SVG support.

Print layouts

Allow 20 mm margins on A4 letters and 12 mm on A6 appointment cards. Use a horizontal logo at least 100 mm wide and keep contact details as separate text.

Use the vector source where supported. Ask the printer for a proof; the supplied assets use RGB rather than a print-specific color profile.
